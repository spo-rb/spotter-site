Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

The short version

  • Spotter is for people aged 16 and over.
  • We collect what Spotter needs to work: your account, your profile, your training and what you share with friends and crews.
  • Only people you accept as friends see your workouts. Anyone else can only find you if they know your exact username, and then they only see your name, username and photo.
  • You choose who sees each workout. Private workouts and your notes are never shown to anyone else.
  • People in your crews see your name, your photo, when you last trained and your chat messages, even if they aren't your friends.
  • If someone bothers you, block or report them in the app. We aim to check reports within 24 hours.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • Your data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Apple handles sign-in and notifications.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from the app.

1. Who we are

Spotter is made by Raza Barati, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact: spotter.supportemail@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an identifier for your Apple Account that is unique to Spotter. We ask Apple for your name only; if you share your first name, we use it to suggest your display name. Apple may also include an email address (your own, or a private relay address if you chose Hide My Email). Our sign-in system stores it with your account, but we don't show it to anyone or use it to contact you.
  • Email and password. A few accounts we set up ourselves (for example, for testing or for Apple's app review) sign in with an email address and password instead. Our sign-in system stores the email with the account and the password only in a scrambled form (a hash) that can't be turned back into the password. We don't show the email to anyone. You can't create an account with an email in the app.
  • Your profile: display name, username, profile photo (if you add one), when you joined and when you last changed your username.

Your training

  • Workouts: name, date, start time and time zone, duration, the time the timer ran, caption, notes, visibility, and where the workout came from (Spotter or an import).
  • Exercises and sets: weights, reps, times, distances, rest times, warm-up and drop sets, and your personal records worked out from them.
  • Custom exercises, routines, notes on exercises, your weekly goal, rest or holiday mode, and your settings (such as default visibility and notification choices).
  • Workout photos you take or choose. Spotter only reads the photo you pick, and saves it as a new JPEG without location or other metadata.
  • Imports from Strong: the CSV file is read on your iPhone; the workouts in it are then saved like any other (as Private).

What you do with other people

  • Friend requests and friendships, emoji reactions and comments on posts, nudges, and people you mute or block.
  • Crews you make or join, invites, crew chat messages, challenges and their results, likes on crew updates.
  • Reports you make, with a copy of what you reported (see section 6).

Your device

  • If you allow notifications, a push token for your iPhone so we can send them through Apple.
  • Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address and time) for security and troubleshooting.

What we don't collect

No advertising or analytics tools, no tracking across apps or websites, no location, no contacts and no Apple Health data. We never sell or rent your data.

4. Who can see what

  • Anyone signed in to Spotter who types your exact username can find you and see your display name, username, profile photo and how many friends you have in common.
  • Your friends see your workouts at the level you choose for each one: Show all (exercises, weights, reps, rest and duration), Exercises only (exercise names, no numbers) or Check-in (just that you trained), with the caption and photo. They also see your weekly goal, whether you're in rest mode and when you last trained, your profile stats and calendar, and leaderboards and head-to-heads built from your Show all workouts.
  • People in your crews (who may not be your friends) see your name and photo, when you last trained, your crew chat messages, your place in challenges, and crew updates about you: personal record milestones on bench press, squat and deadlift (from Show all workouts) and when you haven't trained for a while.
  • Your comments and reactions on a post are seen by everyone who can see that post: the friends of the person who posted it, who may not be your friends.
  • Private workouts and your notes are never shown to anyone else.
  • We (the developer) can access the data on our servers when we need to, for example to review a report, fix a problem or answer your request.
  • We share information with the police or other authorities only when the law requires it, or to protect someone at risk of serious harm (for example, when we report child sexual abuse material).

5. Service providers

We use these providers to run Spotter. They process data on our behalf, under contracts that limit what they can do with it.

  • Supabase: database, file storage, sign-in and server functions. Your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and app distribution. Apple's own handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Some providers may access data from outside the UK. When that happens it's protected by safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum or standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and training data are kept for as long as you have an account.
  • Things you delete (a workout, a routine, an exercise) disappear from your devices and from what anyone else sees straight away, and are erased from our database 30 days later. A custom exercise or routine that your past workouts still use keeps a record marked as deleted until those workouts go, so your history keeps its names. A deleted workout photo is removed from storage straight away. Comments you delete are erased 30 days later.
  • Notifications, crew updates and nudges are deleted after 90 days. Crew chat keeps only each crew's latest 30 messages; older ones are deleted.
  • Reports are kept with a copy of the reported post, comment, message or profile, and what we decided, so we can review them and deal with repeated misuse.
  • Blocks are kept until you unblock the person or either of you deletes your account.
  • Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short time.

7. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. You confirm with Apple (or, for an account that signs in with an email, in the app), and then:

  • Your profile, photos, workouts, routines, settings, friendships, reactions, comments, nudges, crew memberships and chat messages are deleted.
  • Crews you own pass to their longest-standing member, or are deleted if no one else is in them.
  • If you sign in with Apple, Spotter's link to your Apple Account is revoked with Apple.
  • Spotter's data is erased from that iPhone.

A few things stay: reports other people made about your content (with their copy of it, no longer linked to your account), and the results of finished crew challenges, which keep the names and placings of everyone who took part as part of that crew's history.

Logging out erases Spotter's data from the iPhone but keeps your account, so it comes back when you sign in again.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data (access), including in a machine-readable form (portability). You can also export your workouts yourself as a CSV file: Profile → Settings → Data → Export workouts;
  • correct data that's wrong (most of it you can change in the app);
  • delete your data (you can also do this yourself, above);
  • restrict or object to how we use it, where we rely on legitimate interests.

Email spotter.supportemail@gmail.com. We'll answer within one month, and may ask a question or two first to make sure the account is yours. You can withdraw consent to notifications at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

9. Under-16s

Spotter is for people aged 16 and over. We don't ask for your age or date of birth: new users confirm they're 16 or older when they sign up. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

If you're 16 or 17, you get the same protections as everyone: only people you accept as friends see your workouts, nobody can find you without your exact username, and there are no private messages, ads or tracking.

10. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers encrypted (HTTPS). Our database only lets each person read and change their own data and what others have chosen to share with them, and workout photos are kept in private storage that only you and the people you share the workout with can open. Profile photos are stored so that they can be shown to other Spotter users. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you and the ICO if a breach puts you at risk.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or on this site before it takes effect.

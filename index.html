How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. Confirm, then confirm once more with Apple (Face ID or your passcode) if you sign in with Apple. This deletes your profile, photos and every workout on your account for good, and disconnects Spotter from your Apple ID if you used one. It can't be undone.

What happens when I log out?

Logging out erases Spotter's data from that iPhone. Everything that has synced to your account comes back when you sign in again. If something hasn't synced yet (for example, you're offline), Spotter warns you first.

Who can see my workouts?

When you finish a workout you choose who sees it: Private (only you), or your friends with Show all (exercises, weights, reps and rest), Exercises only (exercise names, no numbers) or Check-in (just that you trained). You can change it later from the workout's page, and set a default in Settings → Privacy. Your notes are never shown to anyone else.

How do I report a post, comment, message or person?

Use the ⋯ menu on a post or a friend's profile, or long-press a comment or crew chat message, then choose Report. A reported post, comment or message is hidden for you straight away, and reports go to us to review. The person you report isn't told.

I can't sign in

Spotter uses Sign in with Apple. Make sure you're signed in to your Apple Account in the iPhone's Settings, with two-factor authentication on, and that you have an internet connection. If we gave you an email and password instead, tap Sign in with email and check them for typos. If it still doesn't work, email us.

Can I bring my workouts from Strong?

Yes. Export your data from Strong as a CSV file, then in Spotter go to Profile → Settings → Data → Import from Strong. Imported workouts are saved as Private.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email us with your Spotter username and we'll send you a copy of the data on your account. We may ask a question or two first to make sure the account is yours.
